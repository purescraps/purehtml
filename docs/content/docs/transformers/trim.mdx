---
title: trim
description: Strip leading and trailing whitespace from strings.
---

The `trim` transformer strips all whitespace and newline characters from the start and end of strings.

## Without Trim

<PureHtmlSnippet
  inputHtml="<div>     padded text    </div>"
  configYaml="{ selector: div }"
/>

## With Trim

<PureHtmlSnippet
  inputHtml="<div>     padded text    </div>"
  configYaml="{ selector: div, transform: trim }"
/>
